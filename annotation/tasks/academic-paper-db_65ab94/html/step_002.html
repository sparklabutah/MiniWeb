Modelling Real-time Systems with Bigraphs

Maram Albalwe, Blair Archibald, Michele Sevegnani
2025 cs.LO cs.MA arXiv: 2505.13449

Abstract

Bigraphical Reactive Systems (BRSs) are a graph-rewriting formalism describing systems evolving in two dimensions: spatially, e.g. a person in a room, and non-spatially, e.g. mobile phones communicating regardless of location.
