How to label
  • Judge each primitive from the screenshots, the action log and the requests: did the agent carry out this step as the instruction asks (right item, right values)?
  • The reference is the request the annotator's own walk made. An equivalent request counts; the rubric says what must match and what may differ.
  • report_information: pass when the agent's final answer gives the expected information.
  • A later step that undoes an earlier one fails it. Steps the instruction did not ask for do not fail a primitive unless the rubric says so.
  • Unsure when the evidence cannot tell (e.g. the screenshots stop before the result). Explain in the notes.
  • Cases marked overlap are labeled by every labeler; please do those first.
