This website simulates a local news outlet (Lakeport Tribune) covering a small-city community. The interface is modeled after local newspaper websites like Patch.com, small-town digital newspapers, and local TV news portals.

Data source: data_sources/news/ (articles.json, categories.json, users.json, bookmarks.json)
Searching method: keyword match for search_by_query, bag-of-words overlap for search_by_semantic
