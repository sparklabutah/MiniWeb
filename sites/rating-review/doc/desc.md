This website simulates a Yelp-style local business review platform. Users browse businesses by category, read and write reviews with star ratings, upload photos, and interact socially (save favorites, follow reviewers, report content).

Data source: data_sources/rating-review/ (businesses.json, reviews.json, photos.json, users.json)
All data is set in the fictional town of Lakeport, WA with 25 local businesses across multiple categories.

Key features:
- Business directory with search, category/price/rating filters
- Individual business pages with reviews, photos, and rating distributions
- Review creation with 1-5 star ratings
- Photo gallery with business filtering
- User profiles with review history
- Social features: save businesses, follow users, report content
- Comparison of businesses side by side
- Category-level aggregate statistics

Real-world model: Yelp (yelp.com)
No temporal simulation needed -- reviews and businesses are static user-generated content.
