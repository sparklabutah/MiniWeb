This website simulates a community wiki encyclopedia (Wikipedia/MediaWiki-style) for the fictional city of Lakeport, Washington. The interface is modeled after Wikipedia and Fandom wikis.

Data source: data_sources/wikis/ (pages.json, categories.json, revisions.json, users.json)
Content: 30 wiki articles about Lakeport, Meridian Systems, local landmarks, Pacific Northwest geography, notable people, and community institutions.
Searching method: keyword match and weighted semantic relevance ranking (no external ML models).
Editing: Users can create new articles and edit existing ones with category dropdowns, creating revision history.
