This website simulates a live streaming platform (Twitch-style). The interface supports browsing live and past streams, watching VODs, chatting, clipping, following channels, subscribing, gifting subs, and redeeming channel point rewards. The site is modeled after Twitch.tv.

Streamers: Marcus Chen (coding/gaming), Nathan Brooks (fitness), Natalie Kim (coding), Jake Morrison (startup/building-in-public), Alex Rivera (casual viewer with one board game stream).

Data source: data_sources/live/ (streams.json, users.json, chat_messages.json, clips.json, subscriptions.json, channel_points.json, follows.json, shares.json, reports.json, playback_states.json)
