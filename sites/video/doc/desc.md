This website simulates a video sharing and streaming platform (YouTube). The interface is modeled after YouTube's core experience: browsing, searching, watching videos, interacting through comments, likes, ratings, subscriptions, and managing playlists.

Data source: data_sources/video/ (videos.json, comments.json, users.json, playlists.json, watch_history.json)
Searching method: keyword matching and multi-word semantic scoring (term overlap)
