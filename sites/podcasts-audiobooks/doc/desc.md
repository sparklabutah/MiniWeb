This website simulates a podcast and audiobook streaming platform (Spotify Podcasts / Audible / Apple Podcasts). Users can discover, browse, search, listen to, review, and manage podcasts and audiobooks.

Data source: data_sources/podcasts-audiobooks/ (podcasts.json, episodes.json, audiobooks.json, users.json, library.json, reviews.json)
Searching method: keyword match + semantic keyword-overlap scoring
