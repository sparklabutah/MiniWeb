This website simulates a sports scores and esports platform (ESPN / TheScore / Flashscore). The interface is a multi-sport scoreboard with live scores, standings tables, player directories, team comparison, fan engagement features (favorites, comments, subscriptions), and match highlights.

Data source: data_sources/sports-esports/ (TheSportsDB-inspired synthetic data)
Six leagues: NFL, NBA, MLB, MLS, EPL, Lakeport Esports League
20 teams, 25 players, 30 matches (live/final/scheduled), 5 users
Searching method: keyword-overlap scoring across teams, players, positions, cities
