This website simulates a public transit authority portal (Google Transit / TriMet style) for the fictional city of Lakeport, WA. The interface allows riders to browse bus routes, search for stops, plan trips, check fares, and compare routes.

Data source: data_sources/transit-directions/ (routes_transit.json, stops.json, schedules.json, fares.json, trip_plans.json, users.json)
Trip planning: algorithmic route planner using stop/route graph with support for direct and transfer itineraries
