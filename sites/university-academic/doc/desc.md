This website simulates a university computer science department website (Meridian State University). It provides a course catalog, faculty directory, research area pages, events calendar, and alumni network. The interface is modeled after real university CSE department websites with a navy/gold color scheme.

Data source: data_sources/university-academic/ (courses.json, faculty.json, departments.json, events.json, alumni.json, users.json)

The site supports browsing courses by level and research area, searching faculty and courses by keyword, filtering events by date range and type, comparing courses side-by-side, exporting data as CSV/JSON, submitting applications and contact forms, and subscribing to research area updates. Users can log in with their MSU NetID.
