# Guide: get_nav_route

Get directions between two locations by entering an origin and destination, choosing a travel mode, and requesting the route.

## Preconditions

- The browser is on a map or directions page with 'From' (origin) and 'To' (destination) input fields visible.

## Steps

1. Click on the starting point input field (often labeled 'From' or 'Starting point').
2. Type the starting address or location name.
3. Click on the destination input field (often labeled 'To' or 'Destination').
4. Type the destination address or location name.
5. Select the desired travel mode (e.g., Driving, Walking, Cycling, Transit) by clicking its corresponding button.
6. Click the 'Get Directions' or 'Plan Trip' button to calculate the route.

## Watch for

- The route path highlighting on the map.
- A panel appearing with turn-by-turn directions, total distance, and estimated travel time.

## Pitfalls

- Ensure the correct travel mode is selected before submitting, as some systems do not automatically recalculate when switching modes without re-submitting.
- Be careful to type the full address or select the correct auto-suggested option to avoid routing to an incorrect location with a similar name.

## Variants

- **The destination is already pre-filled from a previous search or map interaction** — Skip entering the destination and only input the starting point before requesting directions.
- **Using a transit-specific trip planner** — Input the travel date and departure time in their respective fields before clicking the plan button.

## Done when

The route is successfully rendered on the map and the estimated travel duration is displayed in the sidebar.
