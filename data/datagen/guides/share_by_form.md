# Guide: share_by_form

Share an item with a specific recipient, platform, or publicly using a share dialog or form.

## Preconditions

- The item to be shared (e.g., article, post, document, conversation) is open or visible on the screen.

## Steps

1. Locate and click the 'Share' button or icon associated with the item.
2. If a share menu or dialog opens, select the desired sharing channel, platform, or recipient.
3. Fill in any required fields in the form, such as selecting a recipient from a dropdown, adding a message, or setting permissions.
4. Click the confirmation button (e.g., 'Share', 'Send', or 'Submit') to complete the action.

## Watch for

- A confirmation message indicating the item has been shared (e.g., 'Shared', 'Shared to Messages', or 'Link Copied').
- The share dialog closing automatically after submission.

## Pitfalls

- Selecting the recipient or platform but closing the dialog before clicking the final 'Share' or 'Send' button.
- Failing to set the correct permissions (e.g., Edit vs. View) when sharing documents with specific users.

## Variants

- **The share options are presented as a dropdown menu next to a button** — Select the target platform or method from the dropdown first, then click the 'Share' button.
- **Sharing with a specific user on a document platform** — Select the user's name, configure their access permissions (e.g., View or Edit), and then submit the form.

## Done when

The share form is submitted and a success confirmation is displayed or the dialog closes.
