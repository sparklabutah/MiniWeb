# Guide: join_meeting

Join a scheduled or code-restricted meeting by entering the meeting code or retrieving its passcode from the meeting details page.

## Preconditions

- The user is logged into the communication platform and has access to either the meeting code or the meetings list.

## Steps

1. If joining directly by code: Navigate to the 'Join' section, enter the meeting code into the designated input field, and click the join button.
2. If joining a scheduled meeting: Navigate to the meetings list, click on the target meeting to open its details page, and locate the passcode.
3. Click the link or button to join the call.
4. If prompted, enter the passcode retrieved from the details page into the passcode input field.
5. Click the final join button to enter the meeting lobby or call.

## Watch for

- An 'Incorrect passcode' error message, which indicates the passcode was entered incorrectly or the meeting ID was mistakenly used instead.
- A 'Joined successfully' confirmation banner or a transition to a 'Connecting...' loading screen.

## Pitfalls

- Confusing the meeting code (e.g., in the URL or meeting title) with the passcode required to enter a passcode-protected meeting.
- Attempting to join a meeting without copying the passcode first, requiring navigation back to the details page.

## Variants

- **The meeting is passcode-protected** — Ensure you copy the passcode from the meeting details page before clicking the join link, then paste/type it into the passcode field on the call screen.

## Done when

The browser redirects to the active call interface or displays a 'Connecting...' status screen for the specific meeting.
