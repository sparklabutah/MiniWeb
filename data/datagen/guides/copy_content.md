# Guide: copy_content

Copy text, code, or a link to the clipboard using an on-screen copy or share control.

## Preconditions

- The target content (such as a code block, translation, or shareable item) is visible on the screen.

## Steps

1. Locate the target content block or item on the page.
2. Find the associated copy control (often labeled 'Copy', 'Copy Link', 'Share', or represented by a clipboard/link icon) near or inside the content container.
3. Click the copy control.
4. If a share menu or modal opens, click the secondary 'Copy' or 'Copy Link' button within that menu.

## Watch for

- The copy button text changing to 'Copied!' or displaying a checkmark icon.
- A temporary toast notification or popup confirming the content has been copied to the clipboard.

## Pitfalls

- Clicking the wrong copy button when multiple content blocks or items are displayed on the same page.
- Assuming the content is copied by just clicking 'Share' without clicking the subsequent 'Copy' option in the dropdown or modal.

## Variants

- **The copy button is not immediately visible** — Hover the mouse cursor over the content block to reveal hidden overlay controls.
- **Copying a URL or sharing link** — Click the 'Share' button first to open the sharing options, then click 'Copy Link'.

## Done when

The copy control displays a confirmed/success state, or a confirmation message appears on screen.
