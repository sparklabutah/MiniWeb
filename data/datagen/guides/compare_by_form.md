# Guide: compare_by_form

Compare two items side-by-side by selecting them from dropdown menus and submitting the comparison form.

## Preconditions

- The comparison page or form is open on the screen.

## Steps

1. Identify the first selection dropdown and click it to open the options.
2. Select the first desired item from the list.
3. Identify the second selection dropdown and click it to open the options.
4. Select the second desired item from the list.
5. Click the submission button (e.g., 'Compare') to generate the comparison view.

## Watch for

- The dropdowns displaying the selected item names.
- The comparison results or side-by-side details rendering on the page.

## Pitfalls

- Selecting the same item in both dropdowns, which may result in an error or a redundant comparison.
- Forgetting to click the submit/compare button if the page does not auto-update.

## Variants

- **The comparison updates automatically upon selecting the items** — Omit clicking the submission button and wait for the comparison view to load.

## Done when

The side-by-side comparison of both selected items is displayed on the screen.
