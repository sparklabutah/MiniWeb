# Guide: authenticate_by_form

Log in to a website by entering a username and password into a sign-in form.

## Preconditions

- The browser is on a page containing a login form, or a page with a visible 'Sign In' or 'Log In' link.

## Steps

1. If the login form is not visible, locate and click the 'Sign In', 'Log In', or 'Login' link/button.
2. Click on the username or email input field.
3. Type the provided username or email address.
4. Click on the password input field.
5. Type the provided password.
6. Click the 'Sign In', 'Log In', or 'Submit' button to submit the form.

## Watch for

- An error message indicating invalid credentials
- The page redirecting to a dashboard or home page upon successful authentication
- The 'Sign In' button changing to a user profile or 'Sign Out' option

## Pitfalls

- Typing the password into the username field or vice versa.
- Clicking 'Sign Up' or 'Register' instead of 'Sign In'.
- Submitting the form before both fields are fully populated.

## Variants

- **The login form is already displayed on the landing page** — Skip the step of clicking the 'Sign In' link and proceed directly to entering credentials.
- **The form fails to submit or shows an error** — Verify the credentials, clear the fields, re-enter the username and password, and try submitting again.

## Done when

The browser redirects to the authenticated home page or dashboard, and the login form is no longer visible.
