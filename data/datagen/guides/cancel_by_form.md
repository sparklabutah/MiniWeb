# Guide: cancel_by_form

Cancel an active booking, order, or appointment through a cancellation form or confirmation dialog.

## Preconditions

- The user is logged in and viewing the list of active bookings, orders, or appointments, or is on the details page of the specific item to be cancelled.

## Steps

1. Locate the specific item (order, appointment, or booking) that needs to be cancelled.
2. Click the 'Cancel' button or link associated with that item.
3. If redirected to a cancellation confirmation page or if a dialog opens, locate the text field for the cancellation reason.
4. Type the required cancellation reason or code into the text field, if specified.
5. Click the final confirmation button (e.g., 'Confirm Cancellation', 'Cancel Order', or 'Cancel Meeting') to submit the request.

## Watch for

- The status of the item changing to 'Cancelled' or disappearing from the active list.
- A confirmation banner or success message indicating the cancellation was successful.

## Pitfalls

- Failing to click the final confirmation button on a multi-step cancellation page, leaving the item active.
- Missing a required cancellation code or reason, which prevents the form from submitting.

## Variants

- **The cancellation is processed instantly upon clicking the initial cancel button** — Skip the confirmation steps and verify the status change directly.
- **A cancellation reason is optional or not requested** — Leave the text field blank and proceed directly to clicking the confirmation button.

## Done when

The item's status is updated to 'Cancelled' on the interface, or a success message is displayed.
