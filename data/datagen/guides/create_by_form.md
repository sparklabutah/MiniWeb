# Guide: create_by_form

Create, register, apply, invite, or post a record by filling out and submitting a form.

## Preconditions

- The user is on the page containing the form, or on a page with a button/link to open the form (e.g., 'Compose', 'Apply', '+ New', 'Create').

## Steps

1. Locate and click the button or link that initiates the creation process (e.g., 'Compose', 'New', 'Apply', 'Create', 'Sign Up').
2. Identify the required and optional input fields, such as text boxes, dropdowns, date pickers, text areas, or file uploaders.
3. Click into each field and enter the specified values by typing text, selecting options from dropdowns, or choosing dates.
4. If required, upload any necessary files by clicking the file attachment button and selecting the file.
5. Locate and click the primary submission button (e.g., 'Send', 'Submit', 'Create', 'Post', 'Save') to submit the form.

## Watch for

- Validation errors (e.g., red text, highlighted fields) indicating missing or incorrectly formatted information.
- A loading spinner or progress bar indicating the submission is processing.

## Pitfalls

- Filling out the form but failing to click the final submit button.
- Leaving required fields blank or failing to replace placeholder text.
- Submitting the form with validation errors, causing the site to reject the creation request.

## Variants

- **The form contains pre-filled information** — Verify the pre-filled details are correct, modify them if necessary, and proceed directly to submission.
- **The form is split into multiple steps or pages** — Fill out the fields on the current page, click 'Next' or 'Continue', and repeat until you reach the final submission button.

## Done when

The form disappears, a success notification is displayed, or the browser redirects to the newly created record, list, or dashboard.
