# Guide: feedback_by_react

React to a piece of content, such as a post, video, or comment, by clicking its corresponding like, upvote, or reaction button.

## Preconditions

- The target content item is visible on the screen, either in a feed, list, or detail view.

## Steps

1. Locate the target content item on the page.
2. Identify the reaction control associated with the item (such as a heart icon, thumbs-up icon, or upvote arrow).
3. Click the reaction control to register your reaction.

## Watch for

- The reaction icon changing color or filling in (e.g., a heart turning red, an arrow turning orange).
- The reaction count next to the icon incrementing by one.

## Pitfalls

- Clicking the reaction button a second time, which will undo/unlike the content.
- Clicking the reaction button of an adjacent post or comment by mistake when scrolling quickly.

## Variants

- **The platform uses upvotes/downvotes instead of likes** — Click the up arrow icon to upvote the content.
- **The platform uses a heart or thumbs-up icon** — Click the heart or thumbs-up icon to like the content.

## Done when

The reaction control transitions to its active, highlighted, or filled state.
