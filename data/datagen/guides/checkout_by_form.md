# Guide: checkout_by_form

Complete the checkout process by navigating to the checkout page, filling out the required shipping, billing, and payment details, and submitting the order.

## Preconditions

- Items or tickets are selected and added to the cart, or the user is on the product/event page ready to purchase.

## Steps

1. Click the checkout button (e.g., 'Proceed to Checkout', 'Go to Checkout', or 'Buy Now') to open the checkout form.
2. Fill in the required contact and shipping information fields (e.g., Full Name, Email, Street Address, City, State, ZIP code).
3. Select the specified shipping method if multiple options are presented.
4. Select the designated payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Checking Account) from the dropdown or radio buttons.
5. Enter the payment details (e.g., Card Number, CVV) if required by the selected payment method.
6. Click the final submission button (e.g., 'Complete Purchase', 'Place Your Order') to submit the form.

## Watch for

- Form validation errors or highlighted fields indicating missing information.
- The order total updating to reflect selected shipping options or applied promo codes.

## Pitfalls

- Submitting the form before all required fields (such as state, zip, or CVV) are fully typed.
- Failing to select the correct payment method dropdown option before entering card details, which may cause validation to fail.

## Variants

- **A promo code is specified in the instructions** — Locate the promo code input field, type the code, and apply it before submitting the order.
- **The checkout is free or pre-authorized** — Skip entering payment details and proceed directly to clicking the final purchase button.

## Done when

An order confirmation page is displayed, or a success message with an order ID appears.
