# Guide: sign_by_text

Sign a digital document by typing your full legal name to adopt a typed signature.

## Preconditions

- The document signing interface is open on the screen.
- The signature options (e.g., Draw vs. Type) are visible.

## Steps

1. Click on the 'Type' tab or button to switch from drawing to typing mode.
2. Click inside the text input field designated for your full legal name.
3. Type your full legal name exactly as required.
4. Verify that the generated signature preview matches your typed name.
5. Click the 'Adopt & Sign' (or 'Adopt and Sign') button to apply the signature and submit.

## Watch for

- The signature preview updating in real-time to show a stylized version of your typed name.
- The 'Adopt & Sign' button becoming active/clickable after typing your name.

## Pitfalls

- Typing the name with typos, which will generate an incorrect signature.
- Accidentally staying on the 'Draw' tab, which will not register the typed text.

## Variants

- **The 'Type' option is already selected by default** — Skip clicking the 'Type' tab and proceed directly to clicking the text input field.

## Done when

The signing modal closes, or the document status updates to 'Signed' or redirects to a confirmation page.
