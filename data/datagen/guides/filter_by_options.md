# Guide: filter_by_options

Narrow down a list of items or search results by selecting specific filter options such as category chips, checkboxes, radio buttons, or tags.

## Preconditions

- A list of items, search results, or a directory is visible on the screen.
- A filtering panel, sidebar, or horizontal bar containing filter options (chips, checkboxes, radio buttons, tags) is present.

## Steps

1. Locate the filter panel, sidebar, or category bar on the page.
2. Identify the target filter option (e.g., a specific category name, tag, checkbox label, or radio button).
3. Click directly on the target option (the chip, checkbox, radio button, or tag link).
4. If the filters do not apply automatically, locate and click the 'Apply', 'Filter', or 'Submit' button to update the results.

## Watch for

- The selected option becomes highlighted, checked, or visually active.
- The list of results updates, reloads, or displays a loading indicator.

## Pitfalls

- Forgetting to click the 'Apply' or 'Submit' button on interfaces that do not auto-update.
- Clicking a parent container instead of the actual interactive checkbox, radio button, or link.
- Selecting the wrong option when multiple options have similar names.

## Variants

- **The filter is a category chip or tag link** — Click directly on the text of the chip or tag to trigger the filter.
- **The filter is a checkbox or radio button** — Click either the checkbox/radio input itself or its associated text label.
- **The interface requires manual submission** — Click the 'Apply' or 'Filter' button after selecting all desired options.

## Done when

The page content or URL updates to reflect the selected filter, and the results list narrows down to match the criteria.
