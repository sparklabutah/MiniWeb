# Guide: edit_by_image

Edit an image via canvas tools

## Preconditions

- The browser is currently displaying a canvas editor, drawing interface, or image editing tool.

## Steps

1. Select the appropriate tool or asset from the sidebar or toolbar (e.g., draw tool, shapes, icons, or filters).
2. Interact with the canvas or properties panel to apply the edit (e.g., click and drag on the canvas to draw/reposition, click an asset to insert it, or adjust sliders/numeric inputs for dimensions and filters).
3. Locate and click the 'Save', 'Apply', or 'Done' button to commit the changes.

## Watch for

- The added element, stroke, or filter visibly appearing on the canvas.
- A confirmation toast or status indicator (e.g., 'Saved' or 'Design saved') appearing after clicking save.

## Pitfalls

- Forgetting to click the save button after completing the edits, causing changes to be lost.
- Attempting to draw or edit without first activating the correct tool mode (e.g., trying to draw while still in selection mode).
- Accidentally selecting and moving the wrong layer or background element on a multi-layered canvas.

## Variants

- **adding pre-made shapes or icons** — Click the desired asset from the library to place it onto the canvas, then drag its handles to resize or reposition it.
- **drawing freehand** — Activate the pen or brush tool, then click and drag across the canvas to create strokes.
- **cropping or adjusting image properties** — Use the properties panel to type in specific dimensions, drag adjustment sliders (like contrast or opacity), or select preset aspect ratios.

## Done when

The canvas displays the updated design and the save action is confirmed by a success message or an updated save button state.
