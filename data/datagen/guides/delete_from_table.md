# Guide: delete_from_table

Delete an item from a list/table

## Preconditions

- The target item or list of items to be deleted is visible on the screen, or accessible via navigation.

## Steps

1. Locate the target item(s) in the list, table, or grid.
2. Identify the deletion trigger. If a direct 'Delete' button, trash icon, or options menu is visible next to the item, click it.
3. If no direct delete option is visible, click on the item to open its detail view, then locate and click the 'Delete' button.
4. If a confirmation prompt, modal, or secondary button appears, click it to confirm the deletion.

## Watch for

- A confirmation dialog or pop-up asking to verify the deletion.
- A temporary status indicator on the item (e.g., 'Deleting...' or a loading spinner).

## Pitfalls

- Failing to click the secondary confirmation button, leaving the item undeleted.
- Accidentally deleting the wrong row due to shifting layouts or misaligned action buttons.
- Navigating away from the page before the deletion request successfully completes.

## Variants

- **Deleting multiple items at once (bulk delete)** — Select the checkboxes next to the target items (or use a 'Select All' option), then click the global 'Delete Selected' button.
- **The delete action is hidden behind an item-specific menu** — Click the 'More options' (three dots) or context menu icon next to the item to reveal and click the 'Delete' option.

## Done when

The deleted item is no longer visible in the list, or a success notification confirms the deletion.
