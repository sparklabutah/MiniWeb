# Guide: sign_by_freeformdrawing

Sign a document or note by drawing a freeform signature on an interactive canvas and adopting/saving it.

## Preconditions

- A signature pad, drawing canvas, or document signing area is visible on the screen.
- The 'Draw' option is selected if there are multiple signing modes (e.g., Draw vs. Type).

## Steps

1. Locate the signature canvas area, often marked with an 'X', 'Sign here', or 'Draw your signature'.
2. Click and drag your mouse cursor across the canvas to draw the first stroke of your signature.
3. Release the mouse button, reposition the cursor, and click-and-drag again to complete subsequent strokes of the signature.
4. Review the drawn signature to ensure it is legible and correctly placed.
5. Locate and click the submission button, typically labeled 'Adopt and Sign', 'Adopt & Sign', or 'Save'.

## Watch for

- Blue or black ink lines appearing on the canvas corresponding to your mouse drags.
- The 'Adopt and Sign' button becoming active or highlighted after drawing.

## Pitfalls

- Accidentally clicking the 'Clear' button instead of 'Adopt and Sign'.
- Failing to release the mouse button between distinct letters, resulting in a single continuous line.
- Attempting to type in a field that requires a drawn signature.

## Variants

- **The interface defaults to typing or another input method** — Click the 'Draw' tab or button above the signature area before drawing.
- **A mistake is made during drawing** — Click the 'Clear' button near the canvas to reset the drawing area and start over.

## Done when

The signature modal closes, the document status updates to 'Signed', or the signature is permanently rendered on the document.
