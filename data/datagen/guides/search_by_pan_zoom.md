# Guide: search_by_pan_zoom

Find content by panning/zooming a map

## Preconditions

- An interactive map interface is visible on the screen.
- The target location, marker, or region is currently off-screen or not clearly visible.

## Steps

1. Click and hold on the map container, then drag in the opposite direction of where you want to go to pan the map.
2. Release the mouse to complete the pan.
3. Locate the zoom controls (typically '+' and '-' buttons) on the map interface.
4. Click the '+' button to zoom in for more detail, or the '-' button to zoom out for a broader view.
5. Repeat the panning and zooming actions until the target location or marker comes into view.

## Watch for

- Map tiles loading and rendering new geographic details as you pan.
- Specific location markers or labels appearing at higher zoom levels.

## Pitfalls

- Panning too quickly, which can cause you to overshoot the target area.
- Zooming in too deep before centering the target, making it easy to get lost on the map.

## Variants

- **Zoom buttons are not easily clickable or available** — Use the mouse scroll wheel (scroll up to zoom in, scroll down to zoom out) while hovering over the map.

## Done when

The target location, marker, or region is centered and clearly visible on the map.
