# Guide: report_information

Report the requested information or answer back to the user once it has been located on the screen.

## Preconditions

- The target information or answer is visible on the current page.

## Steps

1. Locate the specific text, value, or element containing the requested answer on the page.
2. Optionally highlight, select, or click the text/element to confirm or copy the value.
3. Formulate the final response containing the exact answer or value found.

## Watch for

- The correct text or value is fully visible and highlighted if necessary.

## Pitfalls

- Reporting an incorrect or incomplete value due to not scrolling or not verifying the exact details requested.
- Including unnecessary conversational filler or multiple candidate answers instead of a single, clear answer.

## Variants

- **the answer is spread across multiple sections or requires scrolling** — scroll through the page to read and compile all necessary parts of the answer before reporting
- **the answer is a specific number or count** — visually count the matching items on the screen or read the total count directly from the UI

## Done when

The final answer is submitted or reported to the user.
