# Guide: pay_by_form

Fill out a payment or transaction form with required details and submit it to complete a payment or purchase.

## Preconditions

- The browser is open to a page containing a payment, checkout, or transaction form.

## Steps

1. Identify the required fields on the transaction form (e.g., payment type, account number, amount, quantity, or payment method).
2. Select the appropriate options from any dropdown menus (such as payment type or source account).
3. Enter the required alphanumeric details into the text inputs (such as reference numbers, card details, or specific amounts).
4. Review the entered details to ensure accuracy.
5. Click the submission button (e.g., 'Submit Payment', 'Pay Now', 'Buy') to process the transaction.

## Watch for

- Validation errors or red warning text near input fields.
- The submit button becoming active after all required fields are filled.
- A loading spinner or processing overlay appearing after clicking submit.

## Pitfalls

- Submitting the form with incorrect amounts or account numbers.
- Missing required fields which silently prevents the form from submitting.
- Clicking the submit button multiple times while the transaction is processing.

## Variants

- **paying with a credit/debit card** — Fill in the cardholder name, card number, expiration date, and security code (CVV) in their respective fields before submitting.
- **purchasing assets or shares** — Specify the quantity to purchase and select the desired funding account from the available options.

## Done when

A confirmation message, a transaction receipt page, or the status of the item updating to 'Paid' or 'Completed'.
