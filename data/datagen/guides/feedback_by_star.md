# Guide: feedback_by_star

Set a rating value using a slider or star interface and submit the rating.

## Preconditions

- The browser is currently on the detail page of the item to be rated.

## Steps

1. Scroll down the page to locate the rating section, typically labeled with 'Rate', 'Rating', or displaying a set of stars/slider.
2. Adjust the rating control (slider handle or star icons) to match the target rating value.
3. Verify that the displayed numerical value or number of highlighted stars matches the target rating.
4. Click the submission button, often labeled 'Submit Rating', 'Post Review', or 'Submit'.

## Watch for

- The visual indicator (number text or filled stars) updating to the correct value before clicking submit.

## Pitfalls

- Clicking the submit button before the slider or star selection has registered the correct target value.

## Variants

- **The rating interface uses a slider** — Drag the slider handle or click directly on the slider track at the position corresponding to the target value.
- **The rating interface uses star icons** — Click the star corresponding to the target rating number (e.g., click the 4th star for a 4-star rating).

## Done when

The rating submission button becomes disabled, a confirmation message appears, or the page updates to reflect the submitted rating.
