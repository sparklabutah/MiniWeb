# Guide: feedback_by_text

Leave a written review, comment, or thought on a specific item or post.

## Preconditions

- The browser is currently on the detail page of the target item (e.g., a book, photo, or forum post).
- The user is logged in if the platform requires authentication to post comments.

## Steps

1. Scroll down the page to locate the comment or review section.
2. Click inside the text input field or textarea (often placeholder-labeled as 'Add a comment...', 'Write your review...', or 'What are your thoughts?').
3. Type the specified comment or review text into the field.
4. Click the submission button located near the input field (typically labeled 'Post', 'Post Review', or 'Comment').

## Watch for

- The typed text appearing clearly in the input field.
- The submission button becoming enabled or highlighted upon typing.

## Pitfalls

- Clicking a rating submission button (like 'Submit Rating') instead of the text review submission button ('Post Review') when both are present on the same page.
- Failing to scroll far enough to find the correct text input area.

## Variants

- **The input field is a single-line text box** — Click the text box, type the comment, and click 'Post'.
- **The input field is a multi-line textarea** — Click the textarea, type the review, and click 'Post Review' or 'Comment'.

## Done when

The page updates or reloads, and the newly submitted comment or review appears in the list of comments/reviews on the page.
