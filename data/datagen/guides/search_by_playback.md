# Guide: search_by_playback

Locate specific information within an audio or video file by navigating through its playback timeline.

## Preconditions

- A media player interface with a progress or seek bar is visible on the screen.

## Steps

1. Locate the media player's progress or seek bar.
2. Click at different positions along the progress bar to jump to various timestamps in the media.
3. Use the play and pause controls to listen to or view the content at the selected intervals.
4. Repeat seeking and playing until the target information is identified.
5. Pause the media once the information is found.

## Watch for

- The playback head or progress indicator moves to the clicked position on the timeline.
- The audio or video content updates to reflect the new timestamp.

## Pitfalls

- Clicking too far ahead and missing the target segment entirely.
- Not waiting for the media to buffer or load after seeking before clicking again.

## Variants

- **The media player has dedicated forward or backward skip buttons** — Use the skip buttons for incremental adjustments instead of clicking the progress bar directly.

## Done when

The media is paused at the exact timestamp where the required information is revealed.
